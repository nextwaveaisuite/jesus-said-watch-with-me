# ✝️ Jesus said, Watch With Me

> “What, could ye not watch with Me one hour?” — *Matthew 26:40 (KJV)*  

**Jesus said, Watch With Me** is a free, Scripture-based prayer training app that helps believers grow from 15 minutes to one hour in prayer — guided by the Word of God.

## 🌿 Overview
| Level | Duration | Focus |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Level 1 | 15 min | Foundation |
| Level 2 | 30 min | Intercession |
| Level 3 | 45 min | Warfare & depth |
| Level 4 | 60 min | Watch with Christ |

Each level unlocks after the previous is completed.

## 🙏 Features
- AI-style **prayer generator** using KJV Scripture (no API required)  
- Always-visible **Lord’s Prayer**  
- **Timers**, **progress**, **test mode**  
- 100% free and private (no accounts, no data collection)
